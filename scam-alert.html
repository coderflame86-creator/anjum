Online safety notice

Protect yourself from fake offers.

Impersonators may use a creator's name, images, or logo to request money, personal information, passwords, or account access.

Important: Anjum Iqbal does not ask visitors to pay registration fees, share OTPs or passwords, or send money for guaranteed earning opportunities.

Warning signs

Unexpected payment requests

Be cautious of activation fees, VIP access, deposits, release fees, or recovery payments.

Guaranteed profit claims

Promises of fixed daily returns, doubled money, or risk-free results are warning signs.

Urgency and secrecy

Pressure to reply immediately or keep a conversation secret is a common scam tactic.

Passwords and codes

Never share passwords, OTPs, recovery phrases, wallet seeds, or identity documents.

If you are targeted

1. Stop

End the conversation and do not send another payment.

2. Secure

Contact your bank or wallet provider quickly if money or account details were involved.

3. Save evidence

Keep screenshots, usernames, receipts, dates, phone numbers, and transaction references.

4. Report

Report the account through the platform's tools and warn people you trust.
